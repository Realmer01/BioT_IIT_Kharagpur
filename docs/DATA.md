# Data sources, licences and generation

MadhuTwin uses two kinds of data, and never mixes them in reported results.

| | CGMacros (real) | Bharat cohort (synthetic) |
|---|---|---|
| Role | Evidence: training and leave-patients-out validation | Demo patients for the dashboard; tests Indian diets, drugs, sleep and hypoglycaemia |
| People | 45 adults (15 normoglycaemic, 16 prediabetes, 14 T2D), USA | 80 fictional Indian adults (T2D, prediabetes, normoglycaemic) |
| Static record | Age, sex, BMI, weight, height, HbA1c, fasting glucose and insulin, lipid panel | Full EHR: diagnoses (ICD-10/SNOMED), 2-year HbA1c history, labs, medications (ATC), TCF7L2 genotype, polygenic risk, IDRS, consent |
| Streams | Dexcom G6 + Libre Pro CGM, Fitbit heart rate and METs/activity calories, meal logs with macros | 5-min CGM (lag, noise, dropouts), 1-min heart rate, steps, METs, sleep stages, nightly HRV, Indian meal logs |
| Duration | ~10 days each | 14 days each |
| Licence | CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (PhysioNet) | CC BY 4.0 (generated by this repository) |
| In repo? | No: downloaded by script | No: regenerated deterministically by script (seeded) |

## CGMacros (PhysioNet)

> Gutierrez-Osuna, R., Kerr, D., Mortazavi, B., & Das, A. (2025). *CGMacros: a scientific dataset for personalized nutrition and diet monitoring* (version 1.0.0). PhysioNet. https://doi.org/10.13026/3z8q-x658
> Das, A., Kerr, D., Glanz, N., Bevier, W., Santiago, R., Gutierrez-Osuna, R., & Mortazavi, B. *CGMacros: a scientific dataset for personalized nutrition and diet monitoring.* Scientific Data.
> Pollard, T., et al. (2026). PhysioNet as a global platform for biomedical research. *Nature Health*. https://doi.org/10.1038/s44360-026-00096-z

Download (open access, ~630 MB) and checksum verification are scripted in `scripts/download_cgmacros.py`.

**Preprocessing decisions**

1. **Native samples only.** The released 1-minute files linearly interpolate the Dexcom (5-min) and Libre (15-min) readings. An interpolated value at minute *t* depends on the next real reading, which would leak the future into any model. `madhutwin/data/cgmacros.py` recovers the native sampling grid from the interpolation knots and keeps only true sensor samples. A unit test checks this.
2. Dexcom is the primary stream; native Libre samples fill Dexcom gaps longer than 30 minutes.
3. Where the METs column is missing, METs are derived from Fitbit activity calories (1 MET = 1 kcal/kg/h).
4. Meal macros are scaled by the recorded "amount consumed" percentage.
5. Glycaemic group follows HbA1c: <5.7 % normal, 5.7–6.4 % prediabetes, ≥6.5 % T2D.

## Bharat synthetic cohort

Generated by `madhutwin/data/synth_cohort.py` (`python scripts/build_bharat.py`, ~15 s for 80 patients).

**Physiology first.** Each patient gets hidden "true" physiology (insulin sensitivity, beta-cell responsiveness, glucose effectiveness, absorption speed, exercise response, dawn effect). It is sampled from status-specific distributions and modulated by BMI, polygenic risk, TCF7L2 genotype (lower insulin secretion per risk allele) and diabetes duration. Glucose is then simulated minute by minute with the same extended minimal model the twin uses, but with the *true* parameters and inputs.

**Deliberately imperfect observations**, so the twin has to infer what a real deployment cannot see:

- meals are self-logged with carbohydrate-estimation error (log-normal, ~18 %), and some snacks and teas are never logged;
- the twin sees the *prescribed* regimen, while the simulator applies daily adherence (missed doses);
- illness days raise hepatic glucose output and lower insulin sensitivity with no label;
- sleep debt changes insulin sensitivity with a different shape from the twin's own sleep model;
- the CGM has 8-min interstitial lag, AR(1) noise, calibration gain error, and random and long dropouts.

**Population parameters**

- Glycaemic status mix weighted toward T2D (a diabetes-clinic population), with a prediabetes share reflecting India's large prediabetic population (ICMR-INDIAB: 11.4 % diabetes, 15.3 % prediabetes; Anjana et al., *Lancet Diabetes Endocrinol* 2023).
- Regions weighted South 45 %, North 25 %, West 18 %, East 12 %, with city, state and language.
- Indian anthropometrics: sex-specific height, BMI distributions by status, and waist circumference correlated with BMI. Asian-Indian cut-offs are used in the record (BMI ≥23 overweight, ≥25 obese; waist ≥90 cm men, ≥80 cm women).
- Indian Diabetes Risk Score (Mohan et al., *JAPI* 2005), computed from age, waist, activity and family history.
- Prescribing that reflects common Indian practice: metformin (renally adjusted), sulfonylureas (glimepiride, gliclazide), DPP-4 inhibitors (teneligliptin, sitagliptin, vildagliptin), SGLT2 inhibitors, voglibose, pioglitazone, basal insulin glargine. Comorbidity medications include telmisartan, atorvastatin, aspirin and levothyroxine.
- Lifestyle by occupation (IT professional, teacher, farmer, homemaker, retired, driver and others): wake, meal and sleep times, step rates, morning and post-dinner walks, short-sleep nights, late dinners, delayed or skipped lunches, festival or wedding feasts, sweets, tea with sugar.
- Foods: 54 Indian dishes with per-serving macros and glycaemic index (`madhutwin/data/indian_foods.py`). Values are approximate, compiled from the Indian Food Composition Tables (IFCT 2017, ICMR-NIN) and published GI tables. They are simulation inputs, not dietary advice.

**Internally consistent records.** Fasting glucose is the mean simulated glucose in the hour before waking. HbA1c comes from mean glucose via the ADAG relation. HOMA-IR is tied to true insulin sensitivity with measurement noise, and lipids, C-peptide, eGFR, ALT, urine ACR and vitamin B12 (lower with long-term metformin) are correlated with the underlying physiology.

## Sample upload

`data/samples/` holds a fictional patient for the dashboard's **New patient** page: a 6-day CSV of 5-minute CGM, heart rate, steps and logged meals, and a matching health-record form. It is generated by `scripts/make_sample_upload.py` with the Bharat physiology but a seed outside the cohort, so none of the models has seen it. The script picks a sulfonylurea user with frequent highs. Patients uploaded through the page are stored under `data/uploaded/`, which is not versioned.

## What is committed

The repository commits code, metrics (`reports/*.json`) and figures. Apart from the small fictional sample upload, it does **not** commit raw or processed data or trained model binaries: CGMacros is share-alike and non-commercial, and everything else is regenerated deterministically by the scripts in `scripts/`.
